The manager has the design system's navigation sidebar. From `lg` up, a persistent rail holds the brand and the help entry point, **Add Bookmark**, the smart views (**All Bookmarks**, **Weekly Digest**, **Never Opened**, **Untagged**, **Broken Links**) with a count on each and the one on screen marked `aria-current="page"`, the saved views, and the folder tree behind a **Manage folders** switch. **Untagged** is a new filter — also a toggle in the filter strip, and a saved view can hold it. Narrower viewports keep the header's Folders toggle and the saved-view row above the list.
