On windows narrower than `lg`, editing or adding a bookmark uses the mobile mockup's full-page composition instead of the centered dialog: a sticky app bar (back to bookmarks, the title over the bookmark's folder, delete) and a sticky footer with equal-width **Cancel** and **Save Bookmark** actions. The folder becomes a select over the folders that exist with a **No folder** entry — **Custom path…** hands the field back to the `/` syntax for spellings the list doesn't know. Parsed tags preview as `#` chips under the field, and the rating's targets are 44px. From `lg` up the dialog is unchanged.
